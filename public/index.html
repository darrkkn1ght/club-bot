How to Use Club & Quilox Bot Quick Start
1

Sign In to Your LagosLife Account

Use your LagosLife Username & Password (supports 2FA email/app codes) or paste your Session Cookie. If using Cookie login, open lagoslife.app in Chrome/Edge, press F12 → Network → click any /api/ request → copy the cookie header.

2

Pick a Club & Item

Choose from 7 supported venues: Quilox (Club Eko), Ikoyi Golf Club, Lagos Boat Cruise, Eko Hotel & Suites, Playhouse (PH), Hustle & Bustle, and Moscow Underground.

3

Set Quantity, Speed & Auto-Wait

Tap a preset chip (5x, 20x, 50x, 100x) or type any amount up to 500. Choose a safe speed (2.5s, 4.0s, or 6.0s) to avoid server rate limits. Keep Auto-wait if club is closed checked for Quilox and Playhouse — they are open 10:00 PM – 5:00 AM in-game time, and the bot will automatically wait and start buying the moment doors open.

4

Monitor & Control Live

Tap Start Auto-Spending. Every purchase updates your LagosLife save and broadcasts live venue events (like Dorime! or Takeover) to the club room. You can Pause, Resume, or Stop anytime.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why does it say my account is not authorized?

This bot is licensed to a single LagosLife username. Only the licensed account can sign in and run jobs.

Can I close the browser tab while it runs?

Yes, the spender loop runs on the server. Just come back to check progress, pause, or stop.

What happens if my cash runs out?

The bot checks your balance before every purchase and stops safely once you don't have enough cash for the next item.
